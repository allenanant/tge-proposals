The product

A peer-to-peer safety companion. Verified. Women-only. Campus-scoped.

Three behaviors your students already do every night, made faster, structured, and backed by a verified network.

01 · Walk Together

Five-minute walks, paired in real time.

Another verified student is heading the same way, half a block from her. They pair up. Walk together. Optional chat and voice. Mutual rating after every walk. Real-time. Campus-only.

02 · Check-In Timers

The check-in that sends itself.

She sets a timer for the walk home. If she doesn't check in, her trusted circle is pinged with her live location. Snooze, extend, or cancel any time. Location data is purged after 24 hours.

03 · Trusted Circles

Her group text, except it actually shows up.

A private circle of three to ten women she trusts. Share plans, share locations, share the load. Private, opt-in, fully revocable. Student-owned data. No data sold.

Q.01
What happens to student data? Who owns it?
+

The student owns her data. sidexside processes it under a Data Processing Agreement reviewed by your counsel before signing. Location data is purged after 24 hours. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Q.02
How is this different from LiveSafe or Rave Guardian?
+

Those are emergency-response tools, built for the 1% of moments after something has gone wrong. sidexside is an everyday companion, built for the 99%: routine walks, short cab rides, "text me when you're home." Different problem, different product. Most partner campuses run both.

Q.03
Why women-only? Is that legally defensible?
+

Yes. sidexside operates as a private affinity platform, well-established under Title IX as a permissible safety resource targeted at a disproportionately affected population. We share the legal memo with your counsel before signing.

Q.04
What does our Title IX office actually have to do?
+

Operationally, almost nothing. We need one named contact to coordinate the launch email and approve ambassador recruitment. We handle onboarding, training, moderation, and reporting. Your office gets a quarterly PDF that drops straight into compliance documentation.

Q.05
What does the partnership actually cost?
+

It's not a SaaS license. It's a partnership sized to your enrollment, contracted as a one-line institutional spend. We share the number on the discovery call so the proposal you receive matches your reality, not a price card. The pilot semester carries an exit clause: walk away with full data and no fees if it doesn't perform.

Q.06
If the pilot doesn't perform, what happens?
+

You exit. No fees. No contracts to unwind. We send the full pilot data set so you keep everything you learned, even if it's just to convince leadership why a future safety initiative needs to be student-led. We'd rather you walk than stay in a partnership that isn't moving the needle.
