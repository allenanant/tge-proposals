1. The one thing the whole account optimises toward

Not clicks. Not calls. A completed intake form from a family decision-maker in the Phoenix Valley, followed by a consultation that EverKind rates as qualified, followed by a placement. Every choice below serves that path.

Budget reality: senior living search terms in Phoenix cost roughly $8 to $20 a click. At $2,000 to $2,500 a month that is about 130 to 250 clicks. So the account has to be narrow, high-intent, and ruthless with negatives from day one. Breadth comes later, paid for by results.

3. Settings that stop wasted spend before it happens

Location

Maricopa County plus Pinal County edges (Queen Creek, San Tan Valley). Targeting set to presence only, not "presence or interest", so a relative in Ohio searching about Phoenix does not eat the budget until we decide we want them.

Bidding

Weeks 1–2: Maximise clicks with a max CPC cap, to buy data cheaply. Weeks 3–4: Maximise conversions once we have 15+ intake completions. Target CPA only after 30 conversions. Never Target ROAS on this account.

Match types

Phrase and exact only. Broad match is off at launch and stays off until the search-term report proves the account can afford it.

Schedule and devices

All hours at launch, because families search at night. Review by hour after two weeks. Mobile bids left at parity; most of this traffic is a son or daughter on a phone.

Ad extensions

Sitelinks (Assisted living, Memory care, How it works, Start intake), callouts (No cost to families, Local Phoenix advisors, Compare communities), structured snippets (Types: assisted living, memory care, independent living). No call extension at launch, on purpose, per your intake-first process.

What is excluded

Display Network off. Search Partners off. Auto-applied recommendations off. Audience expansion off. Every one of these quietly spends money on the wrong people.

6. The intake-first landing page

We work with your existing site. The change is a dedicated landing path per ad group, each with: one clear promise, three trust lines (no cost, local, we go with you), a short explanation of how EverKind works, and the intake form. The form is multi-step: who you are looking for, care needs, timeline, location, budget band, contact details last. Multi-step forms complete more often and give your team the detail a proper consultation needs.

Two conversion actions are defined: intake_start (someone began the form, secondary, for diagnosis only) and intake_submit (completed, the primary conversion Google optimises to).

7. Tracking: from the ad to the placement and the payment

Google ad click (gclid captured) └─▶ landing page ──GTM──▶ GA4 event: page_view, intake_start └─▶ intake form submitted ├─▶ GA4 event: intake_submit ├─▶ Google Ads conversion: Intake Submitted (primary) │ + Enhanced Conversions (hashed email/phone sent with it) └─▶ HighLevel contact created, gclid + source stored on the record └─▶ pipeline stage moves in HighLevel Consultation Scheduled ─▶ offline conversion: Consultation Active Client ─▶ offline conversion: Qualified Client Placed ─▶ offline conversion: Placement (with value) Payment Status = Paid ─▶ reported, not sent to Google
  • Google Tag Manager holds every tag: GA4, Google Ads conversion, and the gclid-to-form field script. Nothing is hard-coded on the site.
  • Enhanced conversions send hashed first-party data with the intake conversion so Google can match more of the conversions it caused.
  • Offline conversions come from HighLevel. When a stage changes, a workflow writes the gclid, conversion name, time, and value to a sheet that Google Ads imports daily. After a month or two, Google can optimise toward consultations and placements, not only form fills.
  • Reporting is one weekly page: spend, intakes, consultations, qualified clients, placements, and payment status, per ad group. Cost per placement is the number at the top.

8. HighLevel: the work queue, and when automation stops

Pipeline (your stages)

New Intake / Needs Review → Awaiting Contact → Consultation Scheduled → Active Client → Touring / Comparing → Needs Follow-Up → Placed → Inactive / Closed. Placed records carry Payment Status (Paid / Unpaid / Pending), payment date, and method (Check / ACH-EFT / Other) as custom fields.

Every record has

Owner, last-contact date, next-follow-up date, notes, tasks. A saved "Today / Overdue" view shows anything whose next-follow-up date has passed. Mobile app works out of the box.

Automation that helps the human

Instant acknowledgment on intake (email and SMS). Internal task to the owner. A short prospect follow-up sequence while the record sits in Awaiting Contact. Consultation reminders 24h and 2h before.

Automation that stops

The moment a record moves to Active Client, Placed, or Inactive / Closed, every prospect sequence is removed from the contact. Active clients instead get an internal reminder if next-follow-up date is empty or overdue, so no one falls through the cracks. Marketing tags are stripped at Placed and Closed.

We start from our existing HighLevel pipeline and workflow templates, then rename, reorder, and re-word them for EverKind. You pay for configuration, not for rebuilding standard functions.
